# ✅ MINIGAME IMPROVEMENTS - COMPLETED

## All Features Implemented

### ✅ Rarity System
- Calculates rarity based on total base stats
- Common: 0-399 stats
- Uncommon: 400-499 stats  
- Rare: 500-579 stats
- Epic: 580-669 stats
- Legendary: 670+ stats

### ✅ Horizontal Bar UI
- Changed from vertical to horizontal layout
- Indicator moves left to right
- Sweet spot positioned at center (50%)
- Width-based positioning

### ✅ Hold Mechanic
- Changed from tap to hold
- onTouchStart = start moving right
- onTouchEnd = stop, drift left
- Smooth 50ms tick intervals

### ✅ Difficulty Scaling
- Common: +3 progress/tick, 40% sweet spot (Green)
- Uncommon: +2 progress/tick, 30% sweet spot (Blue)
- Rare: +1.5 progress/tick, 25% sweet spot (Purple)
- Epic: +1 progress/tick, 20% sweet spot (Orange)
- Legendary: +0.5 progress/tick, 15% sweet spot (Gold)

### ✅ Visual Feedback
- Rarity label shown in title
- Color-coded sweet spots
- Color-coded progress bars
- Smooth indicator movement

## How to Play
1. Select Pokeball and press THROW BALL
2. Minigame appears with horizontal bar
3. Hold screen to move indicator right
4. Release to let it drift left
5. Keep indicator in colored sweet spot
6. Fill progress to 100% to catch
